1. Smart Home Integration: Tech-Driven Luxury
Why It’s Trending?
Smart home technology is a must-have in Lagos’s luxury market, offering convenience and efficiency.

Key Features:
App-controlled lighting and blinds in Lekki Phase 1 mansions.
Voice assistants (e.g., Google Home) for climate and entertainment control.
Smart thermostats saving up to 30% on cooling costs in Lagos’s heat.


Why It Works in Lagos:
Enhances security with smart locks and CCTV, ideal for gated estates like Pinnock Beach.
Reduces energy bills amid rising tariffs (electricity up 15% in 2024).

Why It Works in Lagos:
Supports local artisans from Oshodi and Lekki markets.
