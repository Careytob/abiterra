# Step-by-Step Guide: Buying Your First Home in Lagos

Buying your first home is an exciting milestone in a vibrant city like Lagos, Nigeria. However, the process can feel overwhelming. This guide breaks down the journey into manageable steps to help you navigate the [Lagos real estate market](/buy) with confidence.

## What Is the First Step to Buying a Home in Lagos?

### Step 1: How to Budget for Your Lagos Home

Before browsing [properties for sale](/buy), understand what you can afford:

- **Assess Your Finances:** Review income, savings, expenses, and debts.
- **Include All Costs:** Beyond the purchase price, budget for:
  - Legal fees
  - Agency fees
  - Surveyor fees
  - Land registration
  - Statutory fees (e.g., Stamp Duty)
  - Renovation costs
- **Mortgage Pre-Approval:** Contact banks for pre-approval to set your borrowing limit.

A clear budget prevents disappointment and streamlines your search.

## Why Does Location Matter in Lagos?

### Step 2: Choosing the Right Neighborhood

Lagos’s neighborhoods impact lifestyle and investment potential:

- **Commute:** Proximity to work or transport links reduces [Lagos traffic](/blog/lagos-traffic-tips) hassles.
- **Amenities:** Look for schools, hospitals, and markets.
- **Infrastructure:** Check roads, drainage, and power supply.
- **Security:** Research neighborhood safety.
- **Lifestyle Fit:** Prefer the Island (Lekki, VI) or Mainland (Ikeja, Yaba)?

Explore [Lagos neighborhoods](/buy) to find your fit.

## How Do You Find Properties in Lagos?

### Step 3: Searching for Your Ideal Home

With a budget and location in mind:

- **Define Needs:** Specify property type (apartment, terrace), bedrooms, and features (e.g., BQ, parking).
- **Use Online Platforms:** Browse [Abiterra listings](/buy) for convenience.
- **Hire Agents:** Reputable agents offer exclusive listings and guidance.

## Who Should You Hire When Buying a Home?

### Step 4: Building Your Support Team

Don’t navigate alone:

- **Real Estate Agent:** Optional, but helpful for viewings and negotiations.
- **Property Lawyer:** Essential for due diligence and contracts.
- **Surveyor:** Verifies property boundaries and survey plans.

## Why Is Due Diligence Critical in Lagos?

### Step 5: Conducting Thorough Due Diligence

Due diligence prevents legal issues:

- **Title Verification:** Confirm seller’s title (C of O, Governor’s Consent) at Lagos Lands Registry.
- **Encumbrances:** Check for mortgages, liens, or disputes.
- **Inspection:** Assess structure, plumbing, and electrical systems.
- **Survey Plan:** Verify coordinates and zoning.

**Never skip due diligence** when [buying property in Lagos](/buy).

## How Do You Negotiate a Property Purchase?

### Step 6: Making an Offer

Once due diligence is clear:

- **Submit Offer:** Via lawyer or agent.
- **Negotiate:** Discuss price and terms.
- **Finalize Agreement:** Proceed to contracts.

## What Are the Legal Steps to Buy a Home?

### Step 7: Signing Contracts and Payment

- **Review Contract:** Lawyer drafts/reviews Contract of Sale.
- **Initial Deposit:** Pay upon signing.
- **Payment Schedule:** Use secure methods (bank transfers).

## What Happens at Closing?

### Step 8: Closing and Handover

- **Final Payment:** Complete payment.
- **Deed of Assignment:** Transfers ownership.
- **Pay Fees:** Cover legal fees, Stamp Duty.
- **Receive Documents:** Get title documents and keys.

## How Do You Secure Your Property Title?

### Step 9: Perfecting Your Title

Secure legal ownership:

- **Governor’s Consent:** Apply for consent.
- **Registration:** Register Deed at Lands Registry.

Perfecting your title ensures future ease.
