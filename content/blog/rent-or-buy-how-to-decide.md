# Rent or Buy? A Simple Framework for Deciding

Whether to rent or buy depends far more on your situation than on any rule of thumb. Work through these questions honestly.

## 1. How long will you stay?

Buying usually comes with large upfront costs, so it tends to make more sense the longer you plan to stay. If you might move within a couple of years, renting often keeps your options open.

## 2. What can you afford, all in?

Compare the full monthly cost of owning (repayments, taxes, insurance, maintenance) with the rent for a similar home. Do not forget the deposit and the one-off purchase costs.

## 3. How stable is your income?

A steady income makes long-term commitments easier. If your income varies, keep a larger emergency fund before buying.

## 4. What do you value?

- **Buying** can offer stability, freedom to customise and a potential long-term asset.
- **Renting** can offer flexibility, lower upfront cost and no responsibility for major repairs.

## 5. What does the local market look like?

Prices, rents and borrowing costs differ from place to place and change over time. Look at current local figures rather than relying on generalisations.

## Putting it together

Write down your answers, then price out both options for your own numbers. If the decision is close, a conversation with an independent financial adviser is worth the time.
